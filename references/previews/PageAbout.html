About

A one-person studio in Omaha that builds digital things — usually to answer a question rather than to fill a brief.

EST. 2026 OMAHA, NE ONE PERSON
THE SHORT VERSION
We make digital things to find out what they want to be. Some become products. Some stay experiments.
Twelve started because the interesting part of building is finding out what a thing is while you build it. What we learn in the Playground ends up in the work we ship, and the work we ship usually started as something half-finished.
01

Build to find out

Most projects here start as a question. The fastest way to answer it is to build a rough version and see what it wants to become.

02

Finish some, publish the rest

Work is what got finished. Playground is everything else, kept visible on purpose — half the value of an experiment is that someone else can see it.

03

Make it usable

An idea that nobody can navigate is not finished. Contrast, focus states, keyboard paths and reduced motion are part of the design, not a pass at the end.
