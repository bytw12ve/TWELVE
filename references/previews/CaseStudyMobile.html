THE 402 — HOME SCREEN
01

The question

Every city app is built for visitors. They rank the same twelve restaurants and are useless by your second week in town. The 402 is built for the opposite person — someone who lives here and has run out of ideas.

That reframing changed almost every decision. No onboarding tour, no "top attractions", no map as the home screen.

02
If you already live somewhere, discovery is not a map problem. It is a boredom problem.

The home screen became a single prompt rather than a directory: one thing, chosen for the day and the weather.

PLACE DETAIL
03

Where it is now

Live, and used mostly by people who know the studio. The next question is whether the weekly hand-correction can be automated without the listings getting worse.
