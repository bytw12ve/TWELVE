THE SHORT VERSION
We make digital things to find out what they want to be. Some become products. Some stay experiments.
Twelve started because the interesting part of building is finding out what a thing is while you build it. What we learn in the Playground ends up in the work we ship.
01

Build to find out

Most projects here start as a question. The fastest way to answer it is to build a rough version and see what it wants to become.

02

Finish some, publish the rest

Work is what got finished. Playground is everything else, kept visible on purpose.

03

Make it usable

An idea nobody can navigate is not finished. Contrast, focus states and reduced motion are part of the design.
